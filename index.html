Rediscover your dreams in the ocean of your mind…

Play as Brie, a burned out artist who becomes a mystical fish and drowns into her imaginary underwater world. She meets a little girl on the surface world who can see her through water puddles.

Take turns controlling the two characters and use their unique abilities to solve puzzles together. Traverse the two mirrored worlds where Brie reveals her artistic past, and unite the two to help her regain her creative spark.

～

Features

Swim in an upside down world!

Explore Brie's mirrored underwater and reach corners that are not reachable on land.

Blow bubbles to collect items!

Bubble her abandoned art piece and send it up through any puddles that bring light underneath.

Jump and splash water puddles to drench the town!

Run around and explore the surface as a child and enjoy the freedom and joy of painting the environment with water.
